# Publication template

Record the source project, audience, format, status, claim sources, and review date.

Use implicit FRAME for articles, posts, and talks. Open with the economic question (Focus). Explain the mechanism (Reason). Ground it in evidence or a labeled illustration (Anchor). Explain who or what is affected (Meaning). Close with a decision, implication, or open question (End). Use five paragraphs for a full post and one paragraph when the channel calls for brevity.

| Format | Include |
| --- | --- |
| Working paper or research note | Question, literature, data, method, results, uncertainty, limitations, and replication |
| Blog or newsletter | Plain-language mechanism, specific example, and link to the supporting analysis |
| LinkedIn or Facebook | One takeaway, one concrete example, and a closing question or implication |
| Policy brief or public comment | Decision-maker, recommendation, alternatives, trade-offs, implementation, and success measure |
| Talk or event | Audience, learning objective, evidence-led outline, and Q&A |
| Forecast or market note | Cutoff, horizon, base case, range, assumptions, and evaluation date |

Review each claim against its source and confirm the draft reflects the strength of the evidence. Record the final version, release date, channel, URL, and subsequent corrections in the source project.
