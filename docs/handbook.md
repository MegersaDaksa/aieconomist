# Operating handbook

## From signal to decision

1. **Monitor.** Track research, data releases, company disclosures, and policy changes. Log the source, date, economic mechanism, and decision the news might change.
2. **Frame the question.** Define the decision, population, time horizon, mechanism, outcome, and comparison. State what evidence would change the conclusion.
3. **Collect evidence.** Record source URL, observation period, release date or vintage, units, coverage, transformations, and access or licensing limitations. Keep restricted inputs outside the public repository. Link to a permitted source or use documented synthetic inputs.
4. **Analyze or experiment.** Document inputs, commands, environment, and output. Before collecting trial data, specify assignment, outcomes, sample target, quality rubric, missing data rules, and stopping rule. Record deviations alongside results.
5. **Advise or advocate.** Identify the decision-maker, policy lever, alternatives, distributional effects, costs, and implementation risks. Name the values behind the recommendation and the evidence that would reverse it.
6. **Publish and revisit.** Review claims against sources, verify numbers and visuals, and record channel, date, version, and URL after release. Log material corrections and update affected formats.

## Evidence and review

Label claims as observed facts, estimates, causal findings, modeled scenarios, forecasts, or judgments. Explain identification assumptions for causal claims. For forecasts, store the information cutoff, horizon, range, and comparison forecast, then score the forecast against a specified data vintage. For a model-assisted analysis, record model version, inputs or prompt where shareable, errors found, and the human checks performed. Keep confidential work, employer data, and personal information out of this public workspace.

## Working rhythm

Use a weekly review to pick a research question and an output worth shipping. Revisit open forecasts and published claims monthly. Review the research agenda quarterly and record decisions to continue, revise, or close projects.
