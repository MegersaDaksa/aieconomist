# AI Economist

An applied economics practice studying how AI changes work, firms, markets, finance, and public policy. It also uses AI in economic analysis, with documented validation and human review.

## Start here

- [Research agenda](docs/research-agenda.md) maps the fields, questions, and methods.
- [Operating handbook](docs/handbook.md) covers monitoring, research, experiments, forecasts, advocacy, publishing, and revisions.
- [Project template](templates/project.md) starts a study or decision brief.
- [Publishing template](templates/publishing.md) turns one evidence base into a paper, article, social post, policy brief, or talk.
- [First project: When does an AI workflow pay for itself?](projects/ai-workflow-economics/README.md) includes a runnable simulation, a field experiment design, a blog draft, a social post, and a policy memo.
